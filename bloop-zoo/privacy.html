Bloop Zoo – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Bloop Zoo ("the game") is made by Potatoes PC. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play. Contact: ayanhussain841@gmail.com.

Your game stays on your phone

The game works offline. Your progress (levels, stars, coins, settings) is saved only in the game's private storage on your phone. We run no servers and the game has no accounts of its own, and we do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location. If your phone's own Google backup is on, Android may back up the game's save file with your other app data.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's ad software on your phone may collect:

DataWhy
Advertising ID and other device identifiersShow ads, limit how often you see them, measure them, prevent fraud
Approximate location (from your IP address)Show ads relevant to your region
App interactions (for example, ads viewed or tapped)Ad measurement and analytics
Diagnostics (crash and performance data of the ad software)Keep ads working

This data goes to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. Data is sent encrypted. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice any time in the game's Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset your advertising ID or limit ad personalisation in your phone's settings. Some rewarded videos are optional: you choose to watch them and get the reward only after the video finishes.

Leaderboards (Google Play Games)

The game has optional leaderboards through Google Play Games Services. If you already use Google Play Games on your phone, Google may sign you in automatically when the game opens; otherwise nothing happens until you tap the trophy button, and you can skip Google's sign-in and keep playing. When you are signed in, your Play Games gamer name and avatar, your Play Games player ID and the best scores you post are processed by Google and shown on the leaderboards to other players. This is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy; we only see the leaderboard results that Google shows to everyone. You can manage or delete your Play Games profile in the Google Play Games app or in your Google Account.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played and to fix problems. It may collect an app instance ID, app interactions (for example, levels played), approximate location and diagnostics. We only see totals, never who you are. Analytics starts only after the consent step, and ad personalisation signals from analytics are turned off.

Children

The game is made for teens and adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game removes all of its data from your phone. We hold no copy of your data. To manage data collected by Google's ad software, use your phone's ad settings or Google's tools. To remove your leaderboard scores, delete your Play Games profile in the Google Play Games app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
